[ artistic manifesto ]

a strategy of temporal condensation and sonic pareidolia. nptnnfrs does not seek to inhabit conventional genres, nor does it yield to the saturation of contemporary consumption.

it operates through an exact economy of resources: extracting the spatial weight of industrial, the physical friction of witch techno, and the spectral melancholy of nightmare pop into an absolute, minimalist sound design.

vocal residues turned into language. the mind reconstructing the phantom signal. engineered and sculpted in 3d psychoacoustic space.
