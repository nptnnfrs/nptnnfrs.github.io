ABOUT

NPTNNFRS is an independent electronic music project from Argentina exploring dark experimental techno, electronic pop, manipulated vocals, atmospheric textures and broken club rhythms. BYE is a micro EP about absence, distortion and emotional residue — four fragments moving through dark club spaces, intimate voices and unstable rhythms. A quiet study of what lingers when something is already gone.
